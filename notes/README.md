## Study Tips

1. **Start with Unit 1** — Foundation concepts (functions, arrays, structures, linked lists)
2. **Unit 2** — Queues, trees, BST (core DSA)
3. **Unit 3** — Advanced trees, heaps, graphs (exam heavy)
4. **Unit 4** — C-specific topics (file I/O, preprocessor)

- ✅ Concepts with tables

- ✅ Revision flashcards
- ✅ Checkpoints for self-assessment
