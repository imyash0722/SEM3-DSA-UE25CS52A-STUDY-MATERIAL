A priority queue is an abstract data type in which each element is associated 
with a priority.
•
A priority queue removes the component with the highest priority first.
•
A priority is a number associated with each element
•
Higher the numeric value, could mean higher the priority. 
•
Or lower the numeric value, could mean higher priority
