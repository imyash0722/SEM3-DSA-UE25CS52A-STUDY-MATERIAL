Week 5 Assignments :
Assignment : L17-L20
queue
priority queue 

------------
1.Reverse a queue so that the front becomes the rear and 
the rear becomes the front. Nodes do not change their position.

2. The component of the queue has an entry time. It could be an integer 
like the epoch time in Unix.
There are two queues which have to be merged based on the entry time.
Provide a function in queue.h and queue.c to support such a merger.

3. There are jobs in the queue to be services which are temporarily 
blocked (may be for IO completion). When their turns come, they will
others to go ahead, but hold the position. When they get unblocked, 
they get serviced.
Can you implement such a variation?

4. implement a priority queue as multilist of queues. There is a list of
queues - one for each priority. Evert new entry will join at the back 
of the queue with the particular priority. Always the first job in the 
highest priority queue shall be serviced.
[degree of difficulty : high; not part of the syllabus]
