Preface 
Dear friends, 
A warm welcome to this course “Data Structures and Applications”. I am N S Kumar. 
I am the anchor for this course. We have a very good team delivering this course. 
We will together assist you in learning this subject well. We will provide you with 
slides, lecture notes, MCQs, short and long questions and answers. Most of these 
shall be shared with you on a weekly basis. 
 
We consider this course as one of the most important courses in Computer Science 
curricula. We feel that mastering data structures and algorithms will not only help 
you in learning other subjects, but also in getting placed in a good company. Most 
companies as on today test your ability to think in terms of data structures and 
algorithms. 
 
You may be wondering whether it is required to learn Data Structures and 
Algorithms in this era of Generative AI. Most of you would have used these tools in 
your programming courses.  
 
As on today, these tools are generating programs. But the companies are not yet 
ready to put them into production before they are checked by human eyes. There 
is a shortage of reviewers in the market. You should be able to read programs, 
debug them, make them effective and efficient. You will enhance your reading skills 
only if you also know how to write programs. 
 
So, we strongly recommend that you avoid using these tools while developing 
programs in this course. We will use the tools when appropriate. We will not only 
encourage you to write programs, but also to read programs.
