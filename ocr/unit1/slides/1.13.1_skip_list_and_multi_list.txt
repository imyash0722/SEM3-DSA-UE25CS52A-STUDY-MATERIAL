Skiplist: Intuition
•
A normal shuttle bus stops at every village and may take a long time.
•
An express bus can skip villages and then a local bus can reach the destination.
•
This idea of skipping portions of a sorted list is called a skip list.

Summary

•
Skiplist: a sorted linked list with multiple levels of forward pointers.
•
Higher skiplist levels provide shortcuts for faster searching.
•
Multilist: a list of lists in which a physical node can participate in 
multiple logical lists.
•
Multilists are useful when the same data needs multiple views or 
relationships.
